# Indian Sign Language (ISL) Resources

## Overview
Indian Sign Language is the predominant sign language used in South Asia. It has its own distinct grammar, syntax, and vocabulary, differing significantly from ASL (American Sign Language).

## ISL vs ASL
- ISL often uses two hands for letters (e.g., A, B, C) whereas ASL heavily relies on one-handed spellings.
- Regional variations exist across India.
